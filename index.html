Your HCIA Results Profile

More than a score.

Your results profile will synthesize patterns across your answers and translate them into a practical conflict-intelligence learning profile.

  • Baseline approach Your ordinary tendencies
  • Conflict activation Your pressure patterns
  • Growth capacity Your pathways forward
  • Learning guidance Practical next steps

Conflict intelligence is not simply knowing what conflict is. It is learning to recognize what happens within us, between us, and around us when disagreement becomes meaningful.

The HCIA is an educational self-reflection system. It is intended to help participants recognize behavioral tendencies without reducing anyone to one fixed personality category.

Instead of producing a simple grade or generic summary, the completed system will examine patterns across multiple answers. It will identify possible strengths, tensions, stress responses, blind spots, and growth opportunities.

Results will be presented through an HCIA Results Profile that can support personal reflection, difficult- conversation preparation, mediation readiness, conflict coaching, leadership development, and continued conflict education.

The HCIA approach

A results profile designed for understanding and growth

The assessment is being built around several permanent principles that govern how questions, scores, and participant results profiles will be developed.

Patterns, Not Labels

Results describe tendencies and interactions. They are not intended to define a participant’s permanent identity.

Context Matters

Conflict behavior may change according to relationship, culture, power, safety, responsibility, and emotional intensity.

Insight Must Lead Somewhere

Every results profile should move beyond description by identifying practical reflection questions, exercises, and learning opportunities.

Ethical boundaries

What the HCIA is—and what it is not

Responsible implementation requires clear limits, transparent claims, voluntary participation, careful data practices, and respect for each participant’s dignity and agency.

Intended Purpose

The HCIA is being developed as an educational conflict self-awareness and interpersonal- development resource.

  • Support reflection and conflict literacy
  • Identify possible behavioral patterns
  • Prepare for difficult conversations
  • Connect participants with learning resources
  • Encourage practical growth without shaming

Important Limitations

The HCIA is not intended to function as a clinical, psychological, forensic, legal, or employment- selection instrument.

  • It does not diagnose mental-health conditions
  • It does not determine legal fitness or capacity
  • It does not predict violence or dangerousness
  • It does not establish relationship compatibility
  • It does not replace qualified professional care

Creator and Project Developer

Robert Harrington Jr.

Robert Harrington Jr. created the Harrington Conflict Intelligence Assessment as part of a broader commitment to conflict literacy, mediation education, self-awareness, and practical learning.

The HCIA draws upon mediation training, conflict-resolution education, observation, reflective practice, and continued study. It is being developed through a structured process that includes pilot testing, participant feedback, professional review, usability evaluation, and ongoing revision.

The project does not rely on authority through title alone. Its credibility must develop through transparency: explaining how the instrument was built, what evidence supports it, what limitations remain, and how each version changes over time.

Current development status

Building the foundation before inviting participation

The first public prototype establishes the HCIA’s identity, purpose, assessment pathways, ethical boundaries, and participant experience. The next development stages will add the assessment engine, scoring architecture, profile generation, feedback tools, and learning recommendations.
